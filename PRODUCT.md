# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Hackathon judges doing a single watch-through of a demo video, plus anyone who clones the repository afterward.

## Product Purpose
A reliable orchestration layer for Bright Data scrapers that proves self-healing capabilities work in real-time. Success is a flawless demonstration of a broken scraper recovering automatically.

## Positioning
"Prove it, don't claim it." A real-time, transparent view of scraper health and autonomous recovery.

## Brand Personality
Technical credibility, a "hacker archive" or terminal log register. Monospace, dark, EXIF/log-metadata aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard patterns. No Inter font, no purple-to-blue gradients, and no cards nested within cards.

## Design Principles
- Functional clarity over persuasive flourish
- Dense, technical presentation of data
- Transparent mechanics (show the work, don't hide it)

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ultra-fast legibility. The interface must read correctly in under 3 seconds per screen for judges skimming the demo video.
